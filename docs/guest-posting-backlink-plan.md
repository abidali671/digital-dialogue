## 1. 30-day plan

### Week 1 — Setup

- [ ] Pick **3 pillar posts** to promote (see Section 5)
- [ ] Create a tracker (Sheet or Notion) with columns: site, URL, niche, contact, pitched date, status, live URL, link type
- [ ] Run Google searches from Section 4 and shortlist **30 sites**
- [ ] Cut to **10 sites** with real posts, real traffic signals, and a clear “write for us” / contact path
- [ ] Prepare 2 writing-sample links (your strongest published posts)

### Week 2 — Pitch

- [ ] Send **10 personalized pitches** (use templates in Section 2)
- [ ] Offer **2–3 title options** per pitch from Section 3
- [ ] Follow up once after **5–7 days** if no reply
- [ ] Log every reply (accepted / revise / no)

### Week 3 — Write and publish

- [ ] Write the first accepted draft (**900–1200 words**, one clear takeaway)
- [ ] Include **1 contextual link** to a relevant Digital Dialogue post
- [ ] Add a short author bio with **1 brand link**
- [ ] Match their house style (headings, length, tone)
- [ ] Ask for a live URL and whether the link is follow/nofollow

### Week 4 — Repeat and promote

- [ ] Share the live guest post on LinkedIn
- [ ] Use that guest post as a new writing sample
- [ ] Pitch **10 more** sites
- [ ] Review: replies, accepts, referral clicks in Analytics

### Ongoing cadence (months 2+)

| Cadence | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Weekly | 5–10 new pitches |
| Monthly | 1–2 published guest posts |
| Monthly | Check referral traffic + Search Console for linked pages |
| Quarterly | Refresh title list and pillar posts |

### Filters that save time

After search, prefer sites that:

- Published a real article in the last 3–6 months
- Have an About / Contact / author pages
- Are not thin “DA 40 guest post for $29” marketplaces
- Allow a contextual or bio link (read their guidelines)

Skip if:

- Every post is a paid guest article with 20 outbound links
- Domain is only “guest posts accepted” with no niche focus
- They demand payment for publication (unless you later choose sponsored — that is ads, not editorial SEO)

## 7. Quality checklist (before you send a draft)

- [ ] Article helps **their** readers even if nobody clicks your link
- [ ] One clear job / takeaway
- [ ] 900–1200 words unless their guide says otherwise
- [ ] No invented stats or fake case studies
- [ ] Max **1–2** links to Digital Dialogue (body + bio)
- [ ] Natural anchor text (brand or topic)
- [ ] No em dash spam / AI filler phrases
- [ ] Author bio: who you are + what Digital Dialogue publishes + one link
- [ ] You saved the live URL in the tracker

## 8. How to measure success

| Signal | Where | When to check |
|--------|-------|----------------|
| Reply / accept rate | Tracker | Weekly |
| Referral sessions | Analytics → Referral | 1–4 weeks after publish |
| Linked page impressions/clicks | Google Search Console | 4–8 weeks |
| Indexed guest URL | `site:host.com/your-post` | 1–2 weeks |

**Realistic expectation:** guest posts rarely spike traffic overnight. They build referral visits and ranking support when the host site is real and the linked page is strong.
